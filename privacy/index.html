Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026 · Last updated September 17, 2026

Faxly (“Faxly,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) lets you send and receive real faxes from your iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Faxly app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Faxly, you agree to this policy.

No account. No tracking. No ads.

The short version

  • You don’t create an account — no name, email, or password is required to use Faxly.
  • We collect only what’s needed to send and receive your faxes and to run your subscription.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not show ads.
  • Your device’s contacts stay on your device — we never upload your address book.

Information we collect

All of the following is used only to make the app work (“App Functionality”), is linked to your device-based identity, and is never used to track you:

DataWhat it isWhy
Phone numbersYour assigned Faxly number, and the sender/recipient numbers on each faxTo route and deliver faxes to and from your number
Fax documentsThe files you send and the faxes you receiveTo transmit, deliver, and let you view your faxes
Device identifierA random per-install ID generated on your deviceTo identify your anonymous, device-based account
User identifierAn account ID (and a recovery ID) assigned by our serviceTo keep your number and history, and restore them if you reinstall
Purchase historyThe App Store transaction/receipt for your subscriptionTo verify your subscription and unlock the app

Your contacts

If you grant Contacts access, Faxly reads your address book only on your device so you can pick someone to fax without typing a number. Your contacts are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. You can revoke this permission any time in iOS Settings.

Camera and photos

If you scan a document or import a photo, the resulting file is used to build the fax you choose to send. We access the camera and your selected photos only for that purpose.

How we use information

  • To send, receive, and display your faxes and their delivery status.
  • To assign and maintain your dedicated fax number.
  • To verify and manage your subscription and entitlement.
  • To send you notifications you’ve enabled (fax arrivals and delivery updates).
  • To provide support, prevent abuse, and keep the service secure and reliable.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help operate Faxly, and only as needed to run the service:

  • Telecommunications / fax carriers — to transmit and receive faxes over the phone network.
  • Cloud hosting & storage providers — to store and move fax documents securely.
  • Apple — processes your subscription purchase and delivers push notifications.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to comply with legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and our service.

Data retention

We keep your fax documents and history so they’re available to you in the app while your account and subscription are active. You can delete individual faxes in the app at any time. If your subscription lapses, your number may be released and associated data may be deleted after a reasonable period. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Security

Faxes are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and documents are stored using access-controlled cloud storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards. Note that faxing itself relies on the public telephone network, which is outside our control.

Children’s privacy

Faxly is not directed to children and is intended for users 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices & rights

  • Turn Contacts, Camera, Photos, or Notifications access on or off in iOS Settings.
  • Delete faxes within the app.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise them, contact us at the address below.

International users

Faxly is operated from the United States. If you use the app from outside the U.S., your information may be processed in the U.S. and other countries where our service providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
